Why small steps matter

Big environmental problems - plastic in the oceans, rising emissions, wasted energy - are solved by many people changing habits, communities organising, and policy catching up. You do not need to be perfect. You just need a next step: swap one item, fix one leak, join one cleanup. EcoSteps gives you tools to find the steps that matter most for you.
