Tip & Bill Split Calculator

Type the bill, pick a tip percentage and choose how many people are sharing. You will see the tip, the total and what each person owes. Tap a preset to try 10%, 15%, 18%, 20% or 25% in one click.

Your result will appear here.

Tipping at a glance

Customs differ a lot. In the United States and Canada, 15-20% of the pre-tax bill is typical in sit-down restaurants, and many people tip a little more for great service. In much of Europe a service charge is already included and rounding up or leaving a few coins is common. In Japan tipping is generally not expected. When in doubt, check the receipt: some places already add a gratuity for large groups.

Tip on the pre-tax amount?

Many people calculate the tip on the bill before sales tax, others on the total. Both are fine; the difference is small. Enter whichever amount you prefer in the bill box.

Splitting fairly

The calculator divides the total evenly. If people ordered very different amounts, run the numbers for each person separately with one person per calculation. The "round up" option makes each share a whole number, which is handy when paying in cash.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

In North America 18-20% is common for good table service. Use the preset buttons to compare.

Do I tip on the tax?

It is a personal choice; many tip on the pre-tax subtotal.

Can I split unevenly?

Calculate each person's own items separately, then add the tip share proportionally.
