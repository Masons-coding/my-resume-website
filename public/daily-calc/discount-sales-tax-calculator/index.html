Discount & Sales Tax Calculator

See what an item really costs after a sale: enter the price, the percent off and the sales tax. Or switch the mode to remove tax from a total and find the price before tax.

Your result will appear here.

Order matters (a little)

In most places sales tax is charged on the discounted price, so a discount saves you tax as well. That is how this calculator works. A few stores apply a coupon after tax; check your receipt if the number differs.

Stacked discounts

Two discounts of 20% and 10% are not 30% off. The second applies to an already-reduced price, giving 28% off in total (0.8 × 0.9 = 0.72). Run the calculator twice to see it, using the first result as the new price.

Removing tax

To find the pre-tax price from a total, divide by 1 + the tax rate. For a $113 total with 13% tax, the pre-tax price is $100 and the tax is $13. Switch the menu to "Remove tax from a total".

Frequently asked questions

Is 50% off then 20% off the same as 70% off?

No, it is 60% off. 0.5 × 0.8 = 0.4 of the original price.

What tax rate should I use?

Use your local rate - for example combined GST/HST/PST in Canada, or state plus local sales tax in the US.

Can I enter a price with tax included?

Yes - switch to "Remove tax from a total".
